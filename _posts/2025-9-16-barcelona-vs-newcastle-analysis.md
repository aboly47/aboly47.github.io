# Team Analysis:

## Barcelona:

### Positives:

We are in very good shape:

We beat Valencia 6-0 in La Liga. Our attack is just clicking, even though we brought rotation players in our attack.

Flick's suspension has overturned, so, he'll be available.

Player for player, we are better, and I would also say our team has better chemistry and "clicks" together better.

We have won more often than Newcastle in previous clashes.

Despite the stadium being St James' Park, we have a good away record and our attack is chef's kiss.

### Negatives:

Some of our key players are missing:

Lamine Yamal with a groin issue.

Frankie de Jong is hopeful at best for returning because of his muscle problems.

Gavi will also probably miss this game, but he *might* come back.

And Balde... brother, what is wrong with you? Are you made out of dry pasta? I love you and when you don't break by touching air, you are very good. you link up brilliantly with Raphinha and take over the left wing, allowing him to cut inside and create chances or score. But, you gotta work on your injury problem mate.

(BTW, It's just a joke, I love Balde, he is a brilliant left back, and I hope he comes back even stronger. don't take it as hate.)

Rashford might start because he's a loanie and Flick wants to give him minutes, but his form hasn't been decent in the few games he's come on. I'm hoping he improves though.

## Newcastle:

### Positives:

They just got their first Premier League win (1-0 vs Wolves), which should help morale.

They made good signings in Elanga and Woltemade.

Home advantage for Newcastle will matter — St James’ Park usually gives them a lift, especially in European nights.

### Negatives:

They have struggled to score in several recent matches. 

Injuries/squad concerns: Joelinton is out, Yoane Wissa injured, Ramsey doubtful.

They jost lost Isak, which means morale might drop because of that.


# Result Prediction!

At the end of the day, I feel like we have a more chance of winning this game. Player for player we are better, and we proved in the Valencia game that we have attacking quality even in our rotation players. We have a good record against Newcastle and also Newcastle have just lost Isak and lost out on transfer targets, which should also give us an edge in morale. If we don't make any stupid errors in the back, (Araújo, please!) we should be able to grind this one out.

my prediction is either 2-0 or 3-1, but I'm leaning more towards 3-1. But also, I think it is possible that we draw the game, but it should be really unlikely.
